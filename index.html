个人简介

我现于中国石油大学（华东）青岛软件学院、计算机科学与技术学院攻读博士学位，师从陈程立诏教授（山东省优青，泰山学者青年专家）。 我的研究兴趣主要集中在深度学习、计算机视觉、视频理解与多模态智能等方向，尤其关注复杂视觉场景下的异常检测、行为理解与智能决策问题。目前以主要作者身份发表 SCI 论文 8 篇。

主要研究方向：视频异常检测、360°全景视频导航、显著目标检测与排序、情绪识别、视线估计等。

Xinyu Liu, Ph.D. candidate at the College of Software & College of Computer Science and Technology, China University of Petroleum (East China) (UPC), supervised by Prof. Chenglizhao Chen. My research interests mainly focus on deep learning, computer vision, video understanding, and multimodal intelligence, with particular emphasis on anomaly detection, behavior understanding, and intelligent decision-making in complex visual scenes. I have published 7 SCI papers.

Research interests also cover Video anomaly detection, 360° panoramic video navigation, salient object detection and ranking, emotion recognition and gaze estimation.
